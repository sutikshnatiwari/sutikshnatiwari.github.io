A Story Rooted in Commerce

Five generations of family business taught me that every decision has a number behind it.

I grew up in Kolkata, India, in a family that has traded fresh produce for over 100 years. Watching my family run that business taught me that margin is the difference between a good year and a bad one, and that every decision has a number behind it.

I studied Accounting & Finance at Jadavpur University. Before I graduated, I co-founded BuyBindass, a D2C business, and built its finance from zero: the general ledger, vendor payment workflows, cost allocation, working capital, and internal controls. Taking it from online sales to a retail outlet and manufacturing taught me what finance looks like when it has to run a real company.

At Nivoda, a Headline-backed B2B diamond marketplace, I managed the financial due diligence for an $11M Series A at a $55M post-money valuation. I also built a multi-currency profitability model across 500+ SKUs that helped lift gross margin from 35% to 52%.

At Acro Service Corp, I owned two annual budgets and forecasts across five businesses generating $700M+ in revenue. I automated variance analysis with SQL, Python, and Power BI, cutting reporting turnaround from seven days to three, and presented results to C-suite leaders.

Now I'm doing my MBA and MS in Digital Technology at BU Questrom, with a Corporate Finance concentration, and I serve as Vice President of the Finance & Investment Club. Since May I've been a Teaching Associate (Internship) at Training The Street, teaching three-statement modeling and DCF valuation to more than 2,000 analysts and associates.
